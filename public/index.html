Settings

Scheduled scans
TreeMap re-scans these folders on a schedule while the app is running, and alerts you when a folder grows past your threshold since the previous scan. Every run is also saved to Trends.
Disk-full forecast
Scheduled scans also project when the disk will fill up (needs 5+ scans across 2+ days). You'll get a notification when the forecast drops below:
days of the disk filling up
Live activity
The treemap's Live mode watches the scanned folder in real time and pauses itself when nothing is happening.
minutes without disk activity
Time Capsule
Before TreeMap deletes anything automatically, it keeps a verified copy here — so you can get it back even after emptying the Trash. Files you delete yourself aren't copied.
days
% of this disk — oldest copies go first, and anything too big to fit is left undeleted rather than deleted unprotected
Reclaim Score
TreeMap ranks files by how safe and worthwhile they are to delete, out of 100. These sliders decide how much each signal counts. Set one to zero to leave it out of the score entirely — that is different from it scoring zero, and a signal TreeMap simply could not read is left out too rather than counted against the file.
Cleanup target
Set how much you are trying to free and the cleanup cart shows a meter filling toward it. Leave it empty for no target — the meter then doesn't appear at all. It only measures what you have staged; nothing is ever deleted to reach it.
Cloud accounts
Scan your own Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive in the same treemap — metadata only, no file contents are ever downloaded, and sign-in tokens never leave this computer. Bring your own (free) API app: create one in the provider's developer console with redirect URI http://127.0.0.1:53682/oauth/callback, then paste its client ID below.
Ignore list
Folders, files or patterns to leave alone. Don't scan skips them entirely (faster scans); Don't suggest keeps them out of Smart Suggestions only. Patterns: a full path, a name like node_modules, or globs like *.iso and ~/projects/**/dist.
How TreeMap measures space
TreeMap measures the space files actually occupy, not just the size they report. This panel shows the difference for the folder you last scanned.
